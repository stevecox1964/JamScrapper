## Decisions locked in this session

- **The killer feature (user's words, "kill zone"):** song sequences that match the mood the
  listener directs with Next, then "duration" — which means *listen time as interest*: a whole
  song played is interest, a song cut short is not. `rando_stats` already records finishes,
  skips, passes, played_seconds.
- **AI mood labels sit on top of audio numbers, not instead of them.** Audio alone was shown
  to be weak; the user said "we can pull in AI to figure this out as well, over time."
- **Fixed mood vocabulary** (20 words) so shared words mean the same thing and distance is
  computable. Do not let it become freeform.
- **Do not weight the picker on `bpm`.** Stored but untrusted.
- **Secrets pattern:** `.env` files + `env.py::load_env()`. `fingerprinter.py` keeps its own
  hand-rolled reader for `backend/.env`; left alone on purpose (surgical change).
- Captions hidden by unloading the module, not by CSS (nothing outside the iframe can).

## Open questions

- **Replace genre overlap in `radio.py` or blend it with mood distance?** Backlog says
  "likely both, weighted, decide with the data." Now the data exists. Suggested first cut:
  mood distance = weighted euclidean over (energy, valence, tension) + Jaccard on mood words;
  keep genre as a small tiebreak; keep the 18% wildcard.
- **What should Next push toward?** Today Next shifts the genre mood counter. With labels, the
  natural reading is "move the target away from the skipped song's (energy, valence, tension)
  and toward songs that were finished recently."
- **Junk catalog rows** ("now playing on pandora - …", "chase ai - …") got mood labels too.
  Delete from `tracks` or leave? Small; harmless until they get picked.

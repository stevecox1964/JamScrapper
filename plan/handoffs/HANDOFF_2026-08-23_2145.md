## What was done this session

**Rando is now the only playing mode** (commit `d2a51b3`)
- Header is `Live | Rando`. The Player button is gone. Anything started from a playlist,
  the library, or history turns Rando on so music keeps going when the queue runs out.
- Fixed the "next next next" bug. The saved queue held 100 songs parked at index 33 and
  `nextTrack()` wrapped modulo, so Next walked 66 already-heard songs then replayed from the
  top. The queue is a history log and is now treated as one: restore marks every entry heard
  and starts at the end, `goForward()` only moves to an unheard song, nothing wraps.
  **66 presses to reach a new song became 1** (verified by simulation against the real state).
- Queue capped at `QUEUE_LIMIT = 60`; it had been growing without bound.
- Repeat guard now reloads from `rando_stats` on startup (`RadioDJ._load_recent`) and holds
  100 instead of 40. It was memory-only and wiped by every restart.

**Thumbs up/down, and the Next split** (commit `d2a51b3`)
- 👍 / 👎 either side of the transport in `PlayerControls.jsx`.
- **Next** = "not right now": shifts the mood only, logged in new `rando_stats.passes`,
  never lowers the song's score. **👎** = "less of this song": `rando_stats.votes`, capped
  at `VOTE_CAP = 3`, and skips. `rando_stats.skips` is frozen history — `_taste()` reads it,
  nothing writes it.
- Next now reports the pass mid-queue, which it never did before.

**The mood is on screen** (commit `d2a51b3`)

  from `/radio/next`, `/radio/vote`, `/radio/skip`.

## Decisions locked in this session

- **No PRs, no branches.** Commit and push straight to `main`. Do not ask again.
  (Saved to auto-memory as `feedback_commit_straight_to_main.md`.)
- **Thumbs up/down are wanted**, reversing the 2026-08-21 "no like button" rule. They stay
  capped so no vote can ban a song outright. Do not argue him out of them.
- **Next ≠ dislike.** Steve: *"sometimes I am not in a mood for a song, but I like the song,
  but I will eject song for next one."* Three columns, three meanings — `passes`, `votes`,
  `skips`. Do not merge them.
- **A blank album beats a guessed one.** The old lookup asked for one recording and took its
  first release, which gave "Man in the Box" → `1992-12-20: Seattle Center Arena` and
  "Guerrilla Radio" → `The Battle of Mexico City`. The rewrite scans every strong match,
  keeps only official Album-type releases that are not live/compilation/soundtrack, takes the
  earliest, and returns `""` when unsure.
- **MusicBrainz over iTunes.** Tested head-to-head on the same 8 songs: MusicBrainz 6/8,
  iTunes 3/8. iTunes caught two MB missed, but is not worth a second source yet.
- **Only pressing noise is stripped from titles.** 37% of catalog titles carry suffixes like
  `(2011 remaster)`. `_strip_version_noise` removes those and retries, but leaves real titles
  alone — `movin' out (anthony's song)`, `(you gotta) fight for your right (to party!)`. 9/9
  on the strip tests.
- **A busy server is not an empty answer.** `MusicBrainzUnavailable` is raised after retries
  with backoff so a 503 is never recorded as "this song has no album".
- **BPM is not trusted.** librosa returned exactly `152.0` for four unrelated songs and called
  Mazzy Star's slowest track 161 BPM. It is stored but nothing scores on it. Energy,
  dynamics, brightness and key all separated songs cleanly.

## Open questions

- **Does BPM get fixed or dropped?** It is the one useless field of the five. Options: tune
  `librosa.beat.beat_track` (wider tempo range, tempogram median), or leave it stored-and-
  ignored and score on the other four. Not yet decided — decide with real data, not on paper.
- **How do signature distance and genre overlap combine in `radio.py`?** Replace genre, or
  blend? Genre still carries information audio does not (a cover sounds like the original but
  sits in a different scene). Deferred to build time, as the backlog says.
- **259 of 624 catalog songs still have no local video file.** Not touched this session. Steve
  has said he wants every video saved. Unclear whether this needs a retry pass or is just the
  natural backlog.
